# App shell

Scope: the app's shell and wallet connection on Robinhood Chain testnet (46630 by default), both themes. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the holder who will not sell, arriving from the landing's "Open the app", and the collateral optimiser. The job is to connect a browser wallet on the right chain and see, before anything else, where their liquidation price will live and what the band is doing right now. Proof is the live band and session read from the chain, never illustrative figures.

Constraints: the landing's world (bone, warm ink, registry navy, Archivo / Inter Tight / Spline Sans Mono, tabular figures, the session seal) through a shared tokens package. Reads only through @tapehouse/sdk. Gain and loss never by colour alone. Reduced motion respected.

Unresolved: account figures arrive with the account surface; the shell holds their place.
