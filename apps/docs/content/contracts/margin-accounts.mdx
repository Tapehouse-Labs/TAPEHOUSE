`MarginAccounts` is where a borrower holds collateral and borrows USDG from the [supply vault](/contracts/supply-vault), which has these accounts as its one borrower. Each address has a cross position and, for any asset it chooses, an isolated position. A position holds Stock Tokens, USDG and WETH, owns a share of the vault's debt, and is margined by the [margin engine](/contracts/margin-engine).

## Read a position

```ts
import { readFileSync } from 'node:fs'
import { createPublicClient, http } from 'viem'
import { accounts, CROSS, parseDeployments } from '@tapehouse/sdk'

const deployments = parseDeployments(JSON.parse(readFileSync('deployments/46630.json', 'utf8')))
const client = createPublicClient({ transport: http('https://rpc.testnet.chain.robinhood.com') })
const account = '0xB986a345655097302cac8b4af6C948FfaF7f55FC'

const { equity, requirement, missing, regime } = await accounts.health(client, deployments, account, CROSS)
const price = await accounts.liquidationPrice(client, deployments, account, CROSS, 'TSLA', 50_000_000n)
```

`CROSS` is `bytes32(0)`. An isolated position is named by its asset's symbol as `bytes32`, which `toBytes32('TSLA')` produces. Equity and requirement are USD with 18 decimals. The liquidation price is USD with 8 decimals.

## Positions and checks

- An asset sits in one position of an account at a time, because a split holding would pay a smaller liquidity add-on than the whole. An isolated position is margined alone, so it gets no diversification and its loss never reaches the cross position.
- Stock Tokens are held in raw units and valued at the low edge of their band, which already carries the token's multiplier. A halted band counts for nothing. WETH counts for 80% of its Chainlink price, and for nothing when that price is over a day old. USDG counts at par.
- Borrowing, and withdrawing from a position in debt, need the position's equity to meet the engine's `currentRequirement` over its Stock Tokens at those prices. A position without Stock Tokens needs only non-negative equity. A position holding USDG does not borrow: it repays with `repayWithCollateral`.
- Deposits and repayments stay open whatever the band, the session and the guardian. Borrowing and withdrawing from a position in debt close for a position holding an asset whose band is halted, whose Stock Token has a multiplier change pending or whose liquidity the engine cannot read; for any position while the L2 sequencer is not settled; and for a position holding Stock Tokens while the band cannot tell the session.
- After a band was last seen halted or with a multiplier change pending, borrowing and withdrawing against it stay closed for `RECOVERY`, 30 minutes, and revert with `AssetRecovering(symbol, until)`. `observe(symbol)` records the sighting.
- From the regular open on the last trading day before a weekend or holiday close until the reopening, a loan or withdrawal must leave gross exposure to Stock Tokens at most `weekendLeverage` over equity.
- While the 24/5 session is closed, each position's debt carries a premium of `premiumRate` basis points a year on top of the vault's interest. A repayment pays the debt first and the premium after.

## Functions

`position` is `CROSS` or an asset's symbol. `account` is the account's owner. Where the table says "Account or authorized", the caller is the account, an address it passed to `setAuthorization`, or an agent with an unexpired mandate.

### Deposit and withdraw

| Function | Who may call | What it does | Key reverts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `deposit(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account)` | Anyone for WETH; account or authorized for USDG, Stock Tokens and basket shares | Moves `amount` of `token` from the caller into the position. Stock Tokens, held or through a basket, count against their caps. | `ZeroAmount`, `UnknownPosition`, `UnsupportedToken`, `AssetInOtherPosition`, `AssetCapExceeded`, `AssetWrittenOff`, `Blocked`, `Unauthorized` |
| `depositWithPermit(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account, uint256 deadline, uint8 v, bytes32 r, bytes32 s)` | As `deposit` | Deposits with an EIP-2612 permit from the caller for the token. A permit already used does not stop the deposit if the allowance stands. | As `deposit` |
| `withdraw(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account, address receiver)` | Account or authorized | Sends collateral to `receiver`. A position in debt must then pass its checks. | `InsufficientCollateral`, `InsufficientMargin`, `WeekendLeverageExceeded`, `BorrowingIsPaused`, `AssetRecovering`, `AssetFrozen`, `Blocked`, `Unauthorized`, `MandateExpired`, `MandateReceiver`, `MandateDebtLimit`, `MandateLeverageLimit` |
| `unwrap(address account, address basket, uint256 shares)` | The account, an authorized address or the liquidator; anyone once the position falls short | Redeems basket shares in the cross position for the Stock Tokens they hold, which the position then holds. Returns the amounts. | `UnsupportedToken`, `Unauthorized`, `AssetWrittenOff` |

### Borrow and repay

| Function | Who may call | What it does | Key reverts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `borrow(bytes32 position, uint256 assets, address account, address receiver)` | Account or authorized | Borrows USDG from the vault to `receiver`. The position must hold no USDG and must then pass its checks. | `ZeroAmount`, `BorrowingIsPaused`, `BorrowingAgainstUsdg`, `DebtCapExceeded`, `WeekendDebtCapExceeded`, `InsufficientMargin`, `WeekendLeverageExceeded`, `SessionUnknown`, `LiquidityUnknown`, `SequencerNotSettled`, `AssetHalted`, `CorporateActionPending`, `AssetRecovering`, `AssetFrozen`, `Unauthorized`, `Mandate*` |
| `repay(bytes32 position, uint256 assets, address account)` | Anyone | Repays up to `assets` of the position's debt with the caller's USDG, then its premium with what is left. Returns what it paid. | USDG's own `ContractPaused()` or `AddressFrozen()` |
| `repayWithCollateral(bytes32 position, uint256 assets, address account)` | Account or authorized | Repays with the USDG the position holds. It needs no check and stays open while an asset is halted. Returns what it paid. | `Unauthorized` |

### Lending the position's Stock Tokens

| Function | Who may call | What it does | Key reverts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lend(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account)` | Account or authorized | Lends the position's Stock Tokens through the asset's stock lending vault for its shares. A position in debt must then pass its checks. | `NoLending`, `UnsupportedToken`, `InsufficientCollateral`, `Blocked`, `BorrowingIsPaused` |
| `unlend(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account)` | Account or authorized | Takes lent tokens back into the holding, as far as the vault can return them. | `OutOfReach(symbol, amount, reachable)` |
| `recall(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account)` | Account or authorized, or the liquidator | Recalls lent tokens. What the vault holds free comes back at once and the rest queues as a ticket. | `NoLending`, `ZeroAmount`, `InsufficientCollateral`, `RecallTooSmall`, `TooManyRecalls` |
| `settle(address account, bytes32 position, address token)` | Anyone | Takes into the holding what the vault holds for the position's next recall. | `NoLending`, `NothingToSettle` |

### Authorization and mandates

| Function | Who may call | What it does | Key reverts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `setAuthorization(address authorized, bool allowed)` | Anyone, for its own account | Lets `authorized` borrow, withdraw, repay with collateral and deposit for the caller. Emits `AuthorizationSet`. | None |
| `setMandate(address agent, uint64 expiresAt, uint128 maxDebt, uint32 maxLeverage)` | Anyone, for its own account | Gives `agent` a bounded authority until `expiresAt`: it may deposit, repay and lend, and borrow and withdraw only to the account itself while the position's debt is at most `maxDebt` USDG and its leverage at most `maxLeverage`, in basis points of at least 10,000. A zero `expiresAt` ends the mandate. | `InvalidMandate` |

An address authorized in full is not bound by a mandate. A call outside a mandate reverts with `MandateDebtLimit`, `MandateLeverageLimit` or `MandateReceiver`, and after `expiresAt` with `MandateExpired`.

### Permissionless upkeep

| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| `observe(bytes32 symbol)` | Records the time when the asset's band is halted or has a multiplier change pending. Reverts with `UnknownAsset`. |
| `accruePremium()` | Accrues the premium up to now and records the closure the band's session shows. Returns the premium index. |
| `sync(bytes32 symbol)` | Brings every position's holding of `symbol` down in proportion when the issuer has burned from the accounts, and syncs the asset's lending vault. Reverts with `UnknownAsset`. |
| `clear(address account, bytes32 position, bytes32 symbol)` | Clears a holding a burn left worth less than a raw unit. Reverts with `HoldingNotEmpty`. |

### Liquidator only

| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| `seize(bytes32 position, address token, uint256 amount, address account, address receiver)` | Takes collateral from a position, whatever its health, from its holding first and then straight from the lending vault. |
| `writeOff(address account, bytes32 position)` | Writes off what an empty position owes. Lenders bear it and the premium is forgiven. Returns what it wrote off. Reverts with `PositionNotEmpty`. |
| `collectFee(uint256 amount)` | Adds the liquidator's fees to the reserve. |

Everything here reverts with `NotLiquidator(caller)` for any other caller.

## Roles and admin powers

- The owner sets the liquidator, backstop and each lending vault once, adds baskets, sets the premium rate (at most 100% a year), withdraws the fee reserve and names the guardian. It cannot move a position's collateral, and the band, engine, vault, caps and reserve share are fixed at deployment.
- The guardian can pause new borrowing and withdrawals from positions in debt. Deposits, repayments, seizures and write-offs stay open.
- The liquidator is the only address that seizes collateral and writes off debt.
- The backstop claims the premium set aside for it. Of each premium paid, `reserveShare` goes to the fee reserve and the rest is set aside for the backstop.
- USDG's and Stock Tokens' issuer can pause tokens, blocklist addresses and burn balances. A paused Stock Token, or one whose issuer has blocklisted the accounts, backs no new risk (`AssetFrozen`). If the issuer burns from the accounts, every holding of the asset falls in proportion once `sync` runs.

## Invariants

- Each position's debt is a share of the vault's debt, kept as borrow shares with Morpho Blue's virtual offsets. Only the accounts repay the vault.
- The accounts hold each asset up to a cap fixed at deployment, and the positions' total debt is capped by `debtCap`, with `weekendDebtCap` bounding a new loan before and across a closure.
- Collateral USDG is never lent.
- A write-off retires the position's borrow shares and the vault writes the same amount off, so lenders bear it and no other position's debt moves.
