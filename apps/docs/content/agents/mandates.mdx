---
title: "Mandates"
description: "Give an agent a bounded authority over a margin account: a time, a debt limit and a leverage limit, with loans and withdrawals sent only to the account."
---

A mandate lets an account give one address, such as an agent's own wallet, a bounded authority over its margin positions until a time. It is the alternative to a full authorization, which lets an address do everything the account can do. The account sets a mandate with `setMandate` on `MarginAccounts`, which the MCP server prepares as `accounts_set_mandate`, and an agent checks where it stands with `accounts_mandate` before it borrows.

## Mandate or authorization

| | `setAuthorization` | `setMandate` |
|---|---|---|
| Borrow | To any address | Only to the account itself, within the limits |
| Withdraw | Any collateral token, to any address | Only to the account itself, within the limits |
| Deposit, repay, lend | Yes | Yes |
| Ends | When the account signs the reverse | At `expiresAt`, or when the account sets `expiresAt` to 0 |
| Limits on debt and leverage | None | `maxDebt` and `maxLeverage` |

An address the account also authorized in full is not bound by a mandate. Give an agent a mandate, not an authorization.

## Give a mandate

The account itself signs. With the MCP server, call `accounts_set_mandate`:

| Input | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `agent` | address | The address that may act within the mandate. Mixed-case checksum required |
| `expiresAt` | integer | When the mandate ends, in Unix seconds, in the future. `0` ends the mandate |
| `maxDebt` | decimal string | The most debt a position may carry, in USDG base units (6 decimals) |
| `maxLeverage` | integer | The highest leverage, gross exposure over equity, in basis points, from 10000 (1x) up |

For example, an agent that may carry up to 500 USDG of debt at up to 2x for a day:

```json
{
  "agent": "0xAgentAddressInMixedCaseChecksumForm",
  "expiresAt": 1893456000,
  "maxDebt": "500000000",
  "maxLeverage": 20000
}
```

The result names the signer, which is the account, lists one call to `setMandate`, and carries `limits`, a sentence saying what the signed call lets the agent do. Show that sentence to the owner. The owner signs and sends the call; the server and the agent do not.

The same call from the TypeScript SDK, with viem, is `accounts.setMandate(deployments, { agent, expiresAt, maxDebt, maxLeverage })`, where `expiresAt` and `maxDebt` are `bigint` and `maxLeverage` is a `number`. It returns the `address`, `abi`, `functionName` and `args` of the call.

`setMandate` reverts with `InvalidMandate` when `expiresAt` is not in the future or `maxLeverage` is below 10,000. A new mandate for the same agent replaces the old one. `MandateSet(account, agent, expiresAt, maxDebt, maxLeverage)` is emitted; an `expiresAt` of 0 emits it with zeros.

## Borrow within a mandate

Before preparing a borrow or a withdrawal for the account, the agent calls `accounts_mandate` with the account, its own address as `agent`, and the position: `CROSS`, the default, or an asset's symbol.

| Output | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `expiresAt`, `maxDebt`, `maxLeverage` | The mandate's terms |
| `reason` | `ok`, `none` (no mandate), `expired`, `debtLimit` or `leverageLimit` |
| `debtRoom` | How much more USDG the agent may borrow under the debt limit |

Each result also names the block it was read at. `reason` is computed in this order: no mandate, expired, debt at the limit, leverage at or above the limit. The SDK's `accounts.mandateStatus` and the contract's `mandateStatus(account, agent, position)` return the same reason and `debtRoom`.

| `reason` | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| `ok` | Borrow no more than `debtRoom`, with `receiver` set to the account |
| `none` | Stop. The owner has not given a mandate |
| `expired` | Stop. The owner must renew it |
| `debtLimit` | Do not borrow. Repaying frees room |
| `leverageLimit` | Do not borrow or withdraw. Repaying or depositing lowers leverage |

A position inside its mandate can still be unsafe, because the limits are the account's and the position is still margined against the band. Before a borrow, call `accounts_liquidation_price` with the amount as `borrowing`, and tell the owner the price. Then prepare `accounts_borrow` with the account as receiver. The agent's wallet signs that call.

## What a mandate allows and what reverts

Until `expiresAt`, an agent under a mandate may deposit Stock Tokens, USDG and baskets' shares, repay with the position's own USDG (`repayWithCollateral`), and lend and unlend, as an authorized address may. It may borrow and withdraw under three conditions, checked after the call's own requirement check:

- the receiver is the account;
- the position's debt after the call is at most `maxDebt`;
- the position's leverage after the call is at most `maxLeverage`.

A position without equity counts as infinite leverage. Prices that move an agent over a limit stop its borrowing and withdrawing and leave it free to deposit and repay.

| Error | Raised when |
|---|---|
| `Unauthorized(caller, account)` | The caller is not the account, has no authorization and has no mandate |
| `MandateExpired(account, agent, expiresAt)` | The mandate's `expiresAt` has passed. Any call that needs the account's authorization reverts, deposits and `repayWithCollateral` included |
| `MandateDebtLimit(debt, maxDebt)` | A loan or withdrawal would leave the position's debt above `maxDebt` |
| `MandateLeverageLimit(leverage, maxLeverage)` | A loan or withdrawal would leave leverage above `maxLeverage` |
| `MandateReceiver(receiver, account)` | A loan or withdrawal names a receiver other than the account |
| `InvalidMandate()` | `setMandate` was called with an expiry in the past or `maxLeverage` below 10,000 |

Every other requirement of the account still applies: the position must meet its requirement at the band's low edge, borrowing stops while an asset is halted or recovering, and the weekend cap holds. A mandate sits on top of those rules and removes nothing from them.

The Tapehouse app says the same in words. A `MandateDebtLimit` reads as borrowing that much would take the position's debt over what its mandate allows, and a `MandateReceiver` as a loan or withdrawal that may go only to the account that gave the mandate.
