Tapehouse lets a holder deposit a whole portfolio of Stock Tokens and borrow against it as one position. Because the position is priced as a portfolio, a diversified one earns more borrowing capacity than the same assets priced one by one, and a concentrated one earns less.

The equity market is closed for most of the week and Stock Tokens trade around the clock. Tapehouse keeps a price band on every Stock Token that stays on chain while the market is shut, says what state it is in (open, closed, degraded or halted), and states a position's liquidation price before anyone signs.

## What is running

Everything in these pages runs on Robinhood Chain's testnet, chain 46630. The header shows the head block of the testnet's public index, read when you opened the page. [Verification](/resources/verification) shows how to check each contract against its source.

<Warning title="Testnet, not audited">
Tapehouse is not audited and has no deployment on a mainnet that holds value. A Stock Token is a debt security of its issuer, not a share: it carries no shareholder rights, redeems for cash only, and is not offered to United States persons. Tapehouse is not a broker.
</Warning>
